# PLANT-LEAF-DISEASE-DETECTION

Plant diseases are a major challenge for farmers worldwide, causing significant crop losses and threatening food security. Early detection is crucial for effective disease management, but traditional methods rely on expert knowledge and manual inspection, which can be time-consuming and impractical on a large scale. This study explores the use of deep learning, specifically Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), to automate plant disease detection from leaf images.
